System Objectives & Problem Statement

Directly addressing core inefficiencies in modern technical recruiting workflows

⚠️ 3. Problem Statement

Recruitment processes often involve manually reviewing a large number of resumes, which is time-consuming and may lead to inconsistent evaluation or overlooking suitable candidates.

Recruiters need an efficient way to identify applicants whose skills, qualifications, education, and experience align with job requirements. HireMatch AI automates this process through standardized, unbiased analysis.

🎯 4. Core Objectives

  • Automate resume screening using Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine-learning techniques.
  • Extract relevant information such as skills, qualifications, education, and work experience from resumes.
  • Compare candidate profiles with job descriptions and calculate transparent matching scores.
  • Rank applicants according to their relevance and provide actionable skill gap analysis.

Machine Learning & NLP Processing Pipeline

How HireMatch AI transforms raw documents into structured talent analytics

Step 1
Document Ingestion

Parses PDF (pdfplumber/pypdf), DOCX, and TXT files. Normalizes whitespace, ligatures, and formatting.

Step 2
Entity Extraction

Extracts candidate contact details, degrees, universities, year intervals, and categorized skills.

Step 3
Semantic Matching

Vectorizes text with TF-IDF n-grams (1, 2) and computes cosine similarity against job descriptions.

Step 4
Composite Scoring

Recruiter-weighted formula combining Skills (45%), Semantic (25%), Experience (20%), and Education (10%).

Step 5
Explainable Ranking

Assigns fit tiers (Top Match, Potential Fit), radar charts, skill gap reports, and custom interview questions.

📊

Interactive Leaderboard

Real-time candidate sorting by overall fit score, cohort statistics, and recruiter filter controls for score and mandatory skills.

🕸️

Multi-Axis Radar Profiler

Visual radar chart comparing candidate scores across Skills, Semantic Alignment, Experience, and Education against target benchmark.

💡

Explainable Skill Gap AI

Instant identification of missing core competencies, candidate strengths, experience deltas, and actionable upskilling advice.

🎙️

Tailored Interview Prompts

Dynamic generation of targeted technical and behavioral interview questions based on matched skills and missing gaps.

⚖️

Recruiter Weight Customizer

Fine-tune the importance of skills overlap vs. years of experience vs. degree level dynamically using sidebar sliders.

📤

Multi-Format Reporting

One-click export of applicant rankings to CSV, structured JSON cohort data, or printable candidate evaluation cards.
